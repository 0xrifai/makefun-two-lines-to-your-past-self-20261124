A program that writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt` for the
make.fun bounty **Two Lines to Your Past Self**.

Line 1 warns my past self about one specific moment — the platform at
Gambir station, before the 6:40 train pulls away. Line 2 says why that
moment mattered and what changed because of it: the silence on that
platform is the reason I now say the hard thing while there is still
time to say it.

The entry was chosen from ten candidate pairs drafted for this bounty,
judged on a real specific moment, a picture concrete enough to see, and
a second line that traces what actually changed instead of repeating
the first.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the two lines (under 280 characters total) to
`/output/entry.txt`.
